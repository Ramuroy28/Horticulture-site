About the initiative

Learning from nature. Applying it in the community.

HortiCare presents our Community Service Project journey in horticulture. It brings together practical exposure, observation and digital learning to encourage responsible plant care and sustainable cultivation.

  • Hands-on horticulture learning
  • Awareness of sustainable cultivation
  • Plant health and crop-care knowledge
  • Digital presentation of project outcomes

Main Project · 6 Weeks

Horticulture

Our main project focuses on practical learning and observation in horticulture, covering plant cultivation, nursery care, soil health, irrigation and sustainable practices.

PROJECT FOCUS 01

Plant cultivation

Understanding plant varieties, growing conditions and basic cultivation practices.

PROJECT FOCUS 02

Nursery practices

Learning propagation, transplanting and routine nursery maintenance.

PROJECT FOCUS 03

Soil & nutrition

Observing soil preparation, organic manure and plant nutrition.

PROJECT FOCUS 04

Irrigation

Understanding watering methods, moisture needs and water conservation.

PROJECT FOCUS 05

Plant health

Identifying common plant stress signs and learning preventive care.

PROJECT FOCUS 06

Sustainable practices

Reflecting on responsible horticulture and environmentally friendly methods.

Mini Project · 2 Weeks

Horticulture Crop Care Guide

A compact digital learning resource that introduces simple, responsible crop-care practices for everyday plant health.

WEEK 01

Know your plant

Explore basic plant needs, suitable growing conditions, soil preparation, sunlight and watering routines.

WEEK 02

Care & maintenance

Learn about nutrition, pruning, monitoring plant health, common problems and sustainable care habits.
