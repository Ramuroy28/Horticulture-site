The finger test

Before you water, push a finger about 5 cm into the soil.

  • Dry and crumbly: water slowly and deeply.
  • Cool and damp: wait a day and check again.
  • Soggy: stop watering and improve drainage.

Pest and disease helper

Pick what you see on the plant. The helper suggests a likely cause and an organic first step.

Neem spray: mix about 5 ml neem oil and a few drops of mild liquid soap into 1 litre of water. Test on a few leaves first, shake well, and spray in the evening. Repeat after 7 days if needed.

Field journal

1 June to 26 July 2026. Six weeks of daily garden work, taken from the weekly reports.

  1. Week 1: Site study and plantingSurveyed beds, prepared soil with farmyard manure, laid drip lines, sowed seeds and transplanted seedlings.
  2. Week 2: Water and nutrientsChecked soil moisture, cleaned drip filters, applied compost and vermicompost, sprayed organic micronutrients and mulched.
  3. Week 3: Pests and diseasesScouted leaf by leaf, sprayed neem oil, removed infected leaves, placed yellow sticky traps.
  4. Week 4: UpkeepWeeded, pruned citrus and star fruit, staked young plants, removed banana suckers and repotted anthurium.
  5. Week 5: HarvestHarvested mature fruit, graded, packed and stored it, kept harvest records and discussed market prices.
  6. Week 6: CommunitySpoke with growers, held an awareness session on organic pest control and water saving on 8 July, and compiled the records.
